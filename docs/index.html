Why lowkey safe

Truly offline

No account, no sync servers, no tracking. Your vault lives only on your device — we can't see it, and we can't leak it.

Serious encryption

AES-256 with the key held in your iPhone's Secure Enclave, unlocked by Face ID or a PIN.

Smarter generation

Random, advanced, passphrase, and pronounceable modes — memorable when you need it, brute-force-proof when you don't.

Password Insights

See reused, similar, and weak passwords at a glance — computed entirely on-device.

What it does

  • Password generator — basic, advanced, passphrase, and pronounceable modes.
  • Encrypted local vault — AES-encrypted storage with the key held in your OS secure enclave.
  • Biometric / PIN lock — optional re-auth on open and after short background timeouts.
  • Insights — strength distribution, reuse detection, and similarity warnings.
  • Portable export/import — encrypted backups can be restored on another device.
